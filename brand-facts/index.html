Canyon Crown Tree Co is a professional tree care company based in Salt Lake City, Utah, founded in 2016 by Dave Behlke, an ISA Certified Arborist. The company provides comprehensive tree services throughout Salt Lake County and surrounding areas.

Company Overview

Canyon Crown Tree Co was established to provide professional arboriculture services to residential and commercial properties in the Salt Lake City metropolitan area. The company operates under the leadership of Dave Behlke, who brings formal forestry education and professional certifications to the local tree care industry.

Leadership

Dave Behlke serves as the owner and lead arborist of Canyon Crown Tree Co. His qualifications include:

  • Forestry degree from Utah State University
  • ISA (International Society of Arboriculture) Certified Arborist
  • OSHA Safety Certified
  • Professional experience in tree care since approximately 2016

Services

Canyon Crown Tree Co provides a full range of arboriculture services:

  • Tree Removal
  • Tree Pruning & Trimming
  • Stump Grinding

Professional Standards

Canyon Crown Tree Co operates according to industry best practices established by the International Society of Arboriculture (ISA). The company's ISA certification ensures adherence to professional standards for tree care, including proper pruning techniques, safety protocols, and environmental stewardship.

Safety Compliance

The company maintains OSHA (Occupational Safety and Health Administration) safety certification, ensuring workplace safety standards are met during all tree care operations. This certification covers proper equipment use, hazard identification, and emergency response procedures.
